Compliance-first backlink discovery, qualification, submission packaging, outreach drafting and backlink monitoring for Pure Peptides Canada.

== Description ==

PPC Link Growth Engine discovers websites that could legitimately link to Pure Peptides Canada, scores them on a transparent 100-point model, locates their real submission pages and editorial contacts, prepares personalised listing packages and outreach emails, submits listings only where explicitly authorised, and monitors every acquired backlink.

* Discovery: rotating search queries, curated Canadian seed catalog, competitor backlink mining, unlinked mentions, broken links.
* Qualification: polite crawling, relevance and risk analysis, SE Ranking / Ahrefs / Semrush / Moz metrics, never fabricated.
* Submissions: form detection, field mapping, verified-fact descriptions, explicit authorisation gates, review queue, listing verification.
* Outreach: eight personalised approaches, CASL/CAN-SPAM identification and unsubscribe, approvals, limits, follow-ups, reply tracking. A new campaign gets drafts at once for every matching prospect.
* Autopilot: one confirmed switch lets the engine approve and send outreach and submit listings without asking first, inside the caps, the send window and every hard limit (no sign-ups, no CAPTCHA bypass, no comment posting, robots.txt, claims filter, CASL/CAN-SPAM, suppression, storefront gate, no paid listings).
* Monitoring: scheduled checks, change and loss detection, provider and GA4 imports, notifications and digests.
* Grow now: one click on the Overview runs every stage once, in order, and lists what happened and what needs you.
* Storefront compliance check: pauses automated submissions and outreach sending while the storefront presents products for consumer use.
* On-site SEO: read-only site audit, IndexNow notifications and internal-link suggestions, applied when you click Apply or, under Autopilot, the best ones automatically (with one-click undo).
* Verified opportunities: 595 researched listing and outreach opportunities bundled with the plugin, imported as owner tasks.
* Link Growth Submitter role: hand the owner tasks to a staff member or assistant who sees only that queue.
* Dashboard: Overview, Prospects, Submissions, Outreach, Backlinks, On-site SEO, Automation, Settings, Logs.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin zip under Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin and activate.
2. Open Link Growth → Settings, review the business profile and add your API keys.
3. Run Discover and Evaluate from the Automation Center, then enable automation.

== Changelog ==

= 1.2.1 =
* Fixed: drafts built before the outreach postal address was changed were refused on every approval, automatic and manual, with "Not approved: Body must contain the sender postal address.", because their compliance footer still carried the old address; automation never retried them and the campaign card said they needed editing. The footer of a message that is not sent yet (draft, approved, scheduled, failed or rejected) now follows the current settings: before every approval, before every send and on every manual save, the "<brand> · <postal address>" line and the unsubscribe line below it are rebuilt from Settings (outreach.postal_address, or the business address under Settings → Site when that is empty, and site.brand_name) and replaced in place. The rest of the email and its unsubscribe link token stay exactly as they were, a missing footer is added, and sent, replied, closed or opted-out messages are never changed. Each rewrite at approval or send is recorded once in the audit log ("outreach.footer_refreshed"). A manual save records no separate entry: its "outreach.message_updated" entry says footer_refreshed: true.
* Fixed: drafts already stuck with "Not approved: Body must contain the sender postal address." (or "… the unsubscribe link.") count as waiting instead of "need editing". Automation approves them wherever it already approves drafts: under Autopilot (on its next run, when Autopilot is switched on, or at once when you save the address or brand in Settings), and without Autopilot in the "Auto:" campaigns when Settings has Outreach autosend on and Require individual approval off (on the next outreach run, or at once when you save Settings). Otherwise they wait for you, and approving them yourself now works.
* Fixed: saving an edited draft whose footer carried an old address added a second footer under the stale one. The stale footer is now replaced whole, also when its address runs over several lines or a blank line was added above the unsubscribe line, and a stale copy left above a current one by 1.2.0 is removed. A footer edited so that it can no longer be recognised (for example the "·" between brand and address replaced) is not guessed at: the save says so, and approval and sending refuse the draft with a note to delete the old line or type the current address into it, instead of mailing the old address above the new one. Such a draft counts as needing an edit and is not retried on every run.
* Fixed: a postal address over 200 characters, or one with an HTML entity or a space before a colon, was shortened or rewritten in the footer, so the draft could never pass the postal address check, and under Autopilot it was retried on every run while the card said "Sending automatically". The footer now carries the address exactly as configured, on one line (a line break becomes ", ").
* Every compliance rule is unchanged: each email still needs the sender identification, the postal address and a working unsubscribe link, and is refused without them.

= 1.2.0 =
* Fixed: creating a campaign showed "Campaign created as a draft." and an empty card, and nothing ever happened to it. A new campaign now gets drafts at once for every eligible prospect whose best opportunity matches its approach (active, conspicuously published contact; not suppressed; outside the contact cool-down; one open message per website; verified-database owner tasks excluded as before). The response reports drafted / eligible / remaining / reasons; the auto outreach stage continues the rest in the background and now also fills campaigns you created, not only the "Auto:" ones. Unsent drafts an "Auto:" campaign holds for the same approach move into your new campaign instead of being skipped as "already exists". When nobody is eligible the card says why in plain words.
* Fixed: creating a campaign from the Messages tab left the message list on screen; the page now switches to Campaigns and shows the new card, and the Messages campaign filter lists the new campaign.
* New: every campaign card shows "What happens next" (waiting for your approval, sending automatically with today's count and the next send window, paused because the sender email cannot receive mail, and so on) and offers "Approve all drafts" (POST /outreach/campaigns/{id}/approve-drafts). The Build drafts picker lists only matching prospects by default (GET /outreach/campaigns/{id}/candidates), with "Show all qualified prospects" to pick others. A draft is refused when the website already has an open message elsewhere, and the reason names that campaign.
* New: Autopilot (Overview and Automation pages; POST /automation/autopilot {enabled, confirm}). On: campaigns and drafts are approved automatically and sent inside the daily/weekly caps and the send window, listings are submitted in "auto" mode wherever every safety gate passes, auto drafting runs. Follow-ups are sent automatically only when reply tracking (the inbound webhook secret) is configured, so nobody who already replied is followed up. Turning it on needs confirm=true (also through POST /settings) and records the consent (user, time, policy version) in its own option and the audit log; turning it off stops automatic sending at once and returns messages Autopilot approved but did not send yet to draft. Off: exactly the previous behaviour of the individual settings. Approvals made by Autopilot are recorded as "system:autopilot".
* Fixed: with outreach autosend on but "Require individual approval" also on, the Outreach page claimed emails were sent without review while nothing was ever approved. The banner now says that nothing is approved automatically, and the send stage records "awaiting_approval".
* New: sender readiness checks that the sender email's domain can receive mail (an MX record; an A record alone, as on a parked domain, does not count; the Reply-To domain is checked too; cached 12 hours, failed lookups retried after 20 minutes; filter ppclg_mail_domain_dns). If it cannot, automatic sending pauses with the reason and the site email is offered when its domain has MX. A missing SPF record is a warning. CASL: a postal address without a postal code or city is flagged, and an empty outreach postal address defaults to the business address under Settings → Site.
* New: outreach.timezone (IANA). Empty means the WordPress time zone, except that a site left on UTC for a Canadian business uses the zone of its province (ON/QC/NU Toronto, MB Winnipeg, SK Regina, AB/NT Edmonton, BC/YT Vancouver, NS/PE/NB Halifax, NL St. John's). The effective zone and the next send window are shown on the Outreach page and in Settings.
* Grow now: the panel and step labels follow Autopilot; under Autopilot "next actions" list only drafts that need editing instead of every draft.
* New: internal links on Autopilot. Setting seo.auto_apply_links (always on under Autopilot) and seo.auto_apply_per_day (default 20, 1–100). The internal_links job (daily while it is on, weekly otherwise) applies the pending suggestions with the highest relevance first (specific anchors, several and early mentions, products ahead of pages), never more than seo.max_links_per_post per item, through the same safeguards as a click (opaque blocks, DOM check, kses must leave the content unchanged, a revision of the previous content first), audit-logged as "system:autopilot" ("system" when only the setting is on). A suggestion the safeguards refuse is left for a person and not retried. POST /seo/links/undo-auto {confirm: true} removes every automatic link still present: an item not edited since gets exactly its previous content back, an edited one loses only the inserted link; undone links are not added again. The On-site SEO page shows "Applied automatically: N" with "Undo automatic links". Off, links are applied only by hand, as before.
* Fix: internal links on sites with plain permalinks ("/?product=slug"). Every such address (and the home page) was treated as the same link target, so after one link in a post the other suggestions in it were closed as "already links to this target" and a post that linked to the home page got no product suggestions at all. Link targets are now told apart by their query too.
* New: discovery without a search key. While Autopilot is on and Settings → Discovery lists no competitors, the engine asks SE Ranking for your competitors at most once every 30 days (one provider lookup, within the provider_lookups limit), leaves out marketplaces, social networks, encyclopedias, news, government, your own domain and any domain that reads as health, medical or pharmacy, and keeps the result in discovery.competitor_domains_auto, shown and removable in Settings → Discovery (a removed domain is never added back). Competitor backlink mining uses your list plus this one, and the discover step says what it found. With no search key and no usable provider, "Needs you" names the one key that unlocks discovery.
* Grow now / Overview: the Autopilot switch now sits in the "Grow your site" panel. Its confirmation lists what runs automatically (emails per day and week, internal links per day, open-form submissions per day, discover / evaluate / monitor) and the limits that stay on. Once on, the panel says "Autopilot is on — nothing needs your approval", the step labels no longer say "for your approval" or "applied only by you", and "Needs you" lists only genuine blockers: a sender that cannot receive mail, no discovery source, provider credits used up, and owner tasks for sites that need an account (with the Link Growth Submitter hint). Search Console, metrics, SEO-issue and link-review reminders are not listed under Autopilot.
* Autopilot safeguards: a sender that is not ready (a parked domain without MX, an incomplete identity) or the contact cool-down no longer marks drafts as "needs editing"; they wait ("Waiting to approve: …") and fixing the sender approves them at once. Switching Autopilot off also returns what a background run already in progress approved afterwards, before anything is sent, and background settings writes never put an older switch back. Without Autopilot, automation never approves drafts in your own campaigns (as in 1.1.0). Under Autopilot your campaigns email without review only sites at or above min_score_auto_queue; follow-ups without reply tracking and drafts to lower-scoring sites wait visibly and are listed in "Needs you", and waiting follow-ups go out once reply tracking is set up. Listing packages that waited for review are re-checked and queued once they pass. Switching seo.auto_apply_links on outside Autopilot needs confirm=true. Competitor backlink mining counts against the provider lookup limit. Refused automatic links stay refused when the list is regenerated, undone links are never pruned, and the daily summary counts closed suggestions apart. The next send window is right in half-hour time zones, failed DNS lookups are retried after 20 minutes instead of on every request, and the Overview and admin notice name the real sender problem.

* New: "Grow now" on the Overview runs every growth stage once as a resumable run (continued by the scheduler when the tab is closed), then shows what happened and what needs you.
* New: storefront compliance check (daily, on growth runs and when products, product terms or menus change). While it blocks and the gate is on, automated submissions become owner tasks and outreach sending pauses; the owner may acknowledge the exact findings.
* New: On-site SEO page with a read-only site audit, IndexNow submissions (production sites only) and internal-link suggestions applied only by an explicit Apply click (the previous version is kept as a revision first).
* New: the bundled verified opportunity database is imported as owner tasks (manual submissions with a listing kit), with tier, kind and condition filters on the Submissions page.
* Evaluation order: prospects that did not come from the verified opportunity import (new discoveries, due re-reviews and automatic re-checks) are evaluated before imported ones; imported prospects are evaluated after them.
* New: "Link Growth Submitter" role for handing owner tasks to a staff member or assistant. It has only the read and ppclg_submit_listings capabilities and sees one menu item, Link Submissions: the open owner tasks and the ones that user marked submitted in the last 14 days. It can mark a task submitted (listing URL and note; recorded in the audit log with the user id) and nothing else; every other page and REST route refuses it. Settings has a card with a link to add such a user. Uninstalling removes the role and the plugin capabilities. Tasks that need the owner's decision are not shown to this role: a rejected, suppressed or on-hold site, text that fails the claims check, a listing that may already have been sent or would be a duplicate, and outreach pitches. Its listing URL must be on the task's site. The role is created on the first page load after an update, even when the plugin was not reactivated.
* Mark submitted: the listing URL must be an http(s) address; anything else is refused instead of being stored. A listing URL on the site's own domain is refused, and listing verification ignores one, because a page on the own site always links to it.
* Settings: new seo, compliance and growth sections. The storefront acknowledgement and the IndexNow key cannot be changed through the settings form; turning the storefront gate off asks for confirmation.
