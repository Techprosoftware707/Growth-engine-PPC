Storefront consistency

On 2026-11-11 the live /shop/ listed product categories Weight Loss & Metabolic, Anti-Aging & Cellular, Pigmentation & Tanning, Cognitive & Neuro, Recovery, Growth, Peptide Skincare, Nasal Sprays and Capsules. It also sold syringes (30 G needle) and bacteriostatic water, and carried tags such as buy-semaglutide-canada.

Health Canada advisory RA-81874 (2026-04-09) names BPC-157, CJC-1295, DSIP, Epitalon, GHK-Cu, HCG, Ipamorelin, KPV, Melanotan I/II, MOTS-C, SS-31, TB-500 and retatrutide, and says "For Research Use Only" labelling "does not make these products legal". A Gowling WLG analysis (Sept 2026) reports a June 2026 Québec Superior Court permanent injunction against a Canadian online peptide retailer.

Every listing and pitch in both databases describes the business as a research-use-only lab-reagent supplier, and reviewing platforms can see the store. Submitting that description while the store presents consumer-use categories would misrepresent the business. Gate: the owner settles the store's presentation with Canadian regulatory counsel, and the description must match the live store on the day of each submission. The gate is written into TOP-NEW-50.md and PENDING-QUEUE.md. Nothing on the store was changed.
