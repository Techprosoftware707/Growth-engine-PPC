## How this list was ranked

- Tiers: wave-1/2 rows keep the tiers calibrated in wave 2. Wave-3, wave-4 and wave-5 rows were tiered on the same scale: **T1** = authority >= 60 and strategic (scientific/lab/chemical/research platform, Canadian, maps/data aggregator, company-intelligence database, AI/GEO discovery surface, .edu/.ca university page); **T2** = authority >= 40; **T3** = authority 15-39 or empty-authority scientific/Canadian; **T4** = authority < 15 or empty-authority generic surface.
- Score = authority, minus 30 per tier step, plus bonuses for scientific, Canadian and data/company/AI-discovery platforms; penalties for no public link, login-only visibility, low relevance, a generic social instance, a link that only renders via JavaScript or needs real volunteer-computing work behind it, a stale or uninvited curated resource page, a bot-walled listing whose link could not be verified, a nofollow/noindex forum profile, or a free path that needs assets PPC may not have yet (original preprints, open tenders, a real software package, founder publications, a public science event, a public SDS library or vulnerability-disclosure policy, a real capstone project). Submission URLs are the verifier-confirmed URLs from the CSV.
- **Suggested order:** do the core citations first (Apple, Bing, OSM, Data Axle, BBB, D-U-N-S). Then publish one open dataset/protocol (Zenodo, figshare, protocols.io, IEEE DataPort) so that the research-profile platforms (Scholar, Semantic Scholar, ROR, Kudos, FAIRsharing) have outputs to point to. Create volunteer-computing teams (World Community Grid, Folding@home) only once a machine is actually contributing work. Pitch the university and library resource pages (wave 5) only once the HPLC and COA guides are final: one personalised email per curator, never a template blast, each approved by the owner first.
