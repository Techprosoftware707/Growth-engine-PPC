- Tiers recalibrated across all rows: **T1** = authority >= 60 and strategic (scientific/lab/chemical, Canadian directory or Canadian government, maps/data aggregator, company-intelligence database, major review platform); **T2** = authority >= 40 (or strategic 40-59); **T3** = authority 15-39 or empty-authority Canadian/scientific/strategic; **T4** = authority < 15 or empty-authority generic surface.
