## Boilerplate background

Based in Toronto and established in 2023, Pure Peptides Canada supplies research peptides and laboratory reagents to Canadian research buyers. It was established in 2023 to give Canadian research buyers a domestic source that avoids international shipping delays. Its Research Library publishes practical guides on peptide storage and handling, HPLC testing, reading a certificate of analysis and evaluating Canadian suppliers. Independent Janoshik Analytical test reports are listed on its Lab Testing page by product, vial size and batch, each carrying a verification key that can be checked on the laboratory's own website. Orders are priced in Canadian dollars and ship tracked from inside Canada by Canada Post Xpresspost, so there are no customs holds or brokerage fees. The Canadian team typically replies within one business day. All research products are sold strictly for in-vitro laboratory research use and are not for human or veterinary use.
